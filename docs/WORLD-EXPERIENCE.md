# Reliant: one experience across the website

## Design read

A complete renovation portfolio for homeowners and commercial clients. Photographic interior scenes and architectural drawing sequences provide the environment; navigation, service information, project evidence and inquiries occupy its foreground. Native CSS, the existing Manrope family, approved CAD monogram, transparent brand assets, pale mineral surfaces and restrained cobalt controls. Design variance 8, motion intensity 7, visual density 3.

The previous site's structure put immersive content above conventional flat sections. This redesign replaces that structure across all public routes. It retains the existing URL tree, metadata, real-project records, publication rules, contact field contract and separate owner dashboard.

## The complete visitor journey

1. **Home:** preview Residential or Commercial, then enter that division. The background previews the selection; the same navigation stays available.
2. **Residential:** arrival, living room, kitchen, bathroom, finished basement and exterior work. Native scrolling or chapter navigation changes the scene. Detail controls move the image framing toward the relevant feature and open its service explanation. Real project links lead directly into the photographic project viewer.
3. **Commercial:** architectural study, glazing, facade materials, completed storefront, interior buildout, flooring and exterior restoration. The first stages seek the existing paused CAD/material video. Completed work uses actual owner-managed photography. Services and project evidence open within the same visual environment.
4. **Projects:** an image-led atlas replaces the long portfolio grid. Selecting a project changes the environment. Residential/commercial filters, direct project links and an index remain available. Selection and filters live in the URL.
5. **Case study:** the actual photographs fill the environment. A filmstrip, previous/next controls, full-photo view and an overview/scope drawer expose the complete project. Photo selection lives in the URL. Related work and the inquiry route continue the journey.
6. **About:** readable foreground chapters explain the company and process while a real project stays behind them.
7. **Contact:** the inquiry panel opens over the environment the visitor was exploring. Direct entry uses an appropriate published project image. The existing inquiry form and owner inbox remain intact.
8. **Privacy:** unchanged legal text occupies a readable foreground surface in the same shell. Owner administration remains a separate functional application.

## The layers and their purpose

- The fixed environmental media stays mounted across route changes. A decoded next image crossfades into place; slow loading does not clear the current scene.
- The room/building is the visual depth. Service panels, the project filmstrip and the chapter rail occupy distinct foreground positions.
- Selecting a material changes crop/zoom before its explanation appears. Closing the explanation restores the chapter framing.
- The shared header and utility footer remain stable. Navigation never depends on discovering a photo hotspot.
- Motion communicates a new room, a new project, a focused detail or an opened panel. No graph paper, cursor theater, auto-rotating models or decorative perpetual movement.

## Media and production boundaries

**New generation credits spent on this redesign: 0.** No new uploads or generation jobs were submitted.

The four residential concept images are existing, separately generated views. They establish a visual family but have inconsistent room connections. Current transitions are chapter changes; they do not claim that a visitor has physically walked through connecting doorways. Basement and exterior chapters use real published projects. The residential one-room motion sample remains separate and is not presented as the tour.

A future continuous residential film needs a coherent route and matching keyframes before generation. Its production sequence is arrival into living, living-room details, passage to kitchen, kitchen details, passage to bathroom, bathroom details, then an explicit transition to real project evidence. Each transition must be checked for stable room geometry, unchanged materials and believable camera travel. A concrete asset list and credit estimate must be reviewed before commissioning that film. This redesign supplies the full interactive site around those eventual assets rather than commissioning another isolated hero clip.

Commercial CAD material is an illustrative architectural interpretation of the Raising Cane's exterior. It is not a construction drawing or historical progress record. The end of that sequence switches to the real finished photograph. Other commercial chapters use actual project photographs; their service scope is drawn from published records.

## SEO, accessibility and owner controls

- Existing route slugs, canonical metadata, Open Graph data, organization schema and real-project schema are preserved.
- All chapter headings and descriptions are in server-rendered HTML. Project and photo links have real destinations. No-JS alternatives retain the content and native navigation.
- Dialogs use Radix focus management and Escape dismissal. Reduced motion removes image transition motion and smooth movement. Mobile exposes text detail actions for image pins that would be cropped away.
- Real project queries remain server-side and publication filtered. Unpublishing the commercial source removes its generated sequence. Empty portfolios do not invent case studies.
- The ContactForm still requires JavaScript for submission, as before. Its API, field order, uploads, storage, consent and error handling were not replaced.
- The prelaunch indexing gate remains in force. No claims of live search rankings or field Core Web Vitals are made.

Verification results are recorded in WORLD-VERIFICATION.json after production browser checks.

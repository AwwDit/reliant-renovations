# Connected apartment: asset decision brief

Reviewed September 7, 2026. This brief supports the visual reset and the choice of an immersive apartment experience. It records source research, not an approved model selection or a completed tour.

A genuine walkthrough needs one coherent 3D environment: floors, walls, door openings, furnishings, and camera movement between rooms. Generated room images, videos, and curved panoramas do not supply that geometry or free movement. They can establish art direction or illustrate a service, but they cannot establish a walkable apartment.

Sketchfab provides [glTF and GLB downloads](https://sketchfab.com/developers/download-api), with account authentication required for downloading. CC-BY assets require visible attribution: model title, author, source link, [CC-BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and an indication of modifications. The exact download package and export must be checked after authenticated acquisition. Blendkit publishes its [plan terms](https://www.blendkit.com/plans/pricing/) and [CC0 license explanation](https://www.blendkit.com/docs/licenses/).

The two Sketchfab archive sizes were not exposed by the public metadata inspected. No final web download weight has been measured. A source render is evidence of the artist's presentation, not proof that its lighting, reflections, baked maps, or shaders will look the same in Three.js. Source file size also does not predict the final optimized payload reliably.

The [Luxury One Bedroom Apartment on TurboSquid](https://www.turbosquid.com/3d-models/3d-luxury-one-bedroom-apartment-model-1771186) was considered but is not a clean public-GLB choice: its [standard license](https://www.turbosquid.com/licensing) restricts open-format WebGL distribution and calls for advance approval for other WebGL applications. It was not purchased.

## Higgsfield scene authoring

The installed `scene_builder_3d` tool contracts describe a route to an authored, connected scene: `run_python` exposes Blender 5.2 and `bpy`, with metre-scale editable geometry, descriptive objects, materials, cameras, and lighting. `get_glb` downloads a committed GLB export; `get_blend` downloads the editable scene. Portable Principled materials and embedded textures are supported, while procedural shaders and world lighting may not transfer faithfully.

`search_assets` and `import_asset` support a curated GLB catalog. Specific furniture inventory was not verified because catalog search requires a selected project. The import contract accepts confirmed catalog assets, not arbitrary uploaded or generated model IDs.

The documented workflow also requires `scene_builder_3d_query_python` for inspection and `scene_builder_3d_show_scene` for final delivery. Both exact tools were absent from the active tool inventory and not callable. The authoring/export capability is therefore supported by the available contracts, but the complete workflow has not been demonstrated in this session.

Two read-only model recommendation queries returned Meshy, Hunyuan3D, and Tripo mesh generators. None of the returned contracts guaranteed connected rooms, traversable openings, or a consistent apartment floor plan. This does not establish that no such model exists elsewhere; it establishes that a dedicated connected-world generator was not verified here.

## Production decision

The clearest next evaluation is an authenticated download and local inspection of the Modern Apartment candidate, followed by a small render and navigation prototype. The Studio can answer the narrower movement question with less geometry. An accepted model would still need camera controls, collision or walkable-area constraints, meaningful service hotspots, mobile interaction, accessible alternatives, and measured loading performance.

Any selected stock apartment or newly authored environment must be presented as an illustrative concept. It is not a reconstruction of a Reliant project: the supplied project sources do not establish a measured apartment floor plan.

Research completed: primary listings and tool contracts inspected; two small public preview images saved temporarily for visual review. **No model archive was downloaded, no purchase was made, no remote scene was created or edited, and no completed 3D apartment exists from this research.**

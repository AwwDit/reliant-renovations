# Reliant redesign · September 9, 2026

The latest user direction supersedes the CAD and room-tour concepts. The public website is now a photographic renovation portfolio. Actual work supplies the imagery, with dimensional photo framing, deliberate overlap, mixed image scale, soft controls and a custom floating navigation system. No generated buildings, CAD studies, simulated rooms or virtual tours belong in this direction.

The company remains a commercial and residential general contractor. Both divisions receive clear homepage entry points. All ten documented projects, eighty curated images and sixty-eight scope entries remain in the live owner-managed database.

Visual system: Manrope display at 600–700 weight, paper/smoke surfaces, charcoal text and restrained blue accents. Photo frames use 12–18px corners and directional shadows. Buttons use a capsule body and separate circular arrow. Navigation is composed of a logo tab, route rail and contact control, with real project imagery in its expanded menu. Dark mode uses corresponding charcoal surfaces and light text.

Depth comes from real photographs and compositional layers. Entrance/reveal motion establishes hierarchy; pointer motion adjusts the angle of the photographic hero stack. Natural document scrolling, reduced motion, keyboard access and no-JavaScript content remain available.

The supplied designer package remains the source for company facts, all project scopes, contact fields, service areas and privacy requirements. Its launch/account checklist is not authority to change external credentials or publish the site during this local redesign.
